Spry privacy policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Spry is a brain, word and math training app made by ubecat studio (“we”). This policy explains what Spry stores, what leaves your phone, and the choices you have.

What stays on your phone

Spry has no accounts and no Spry servers. Everything below is stored only on your device:

  • your scores, levels, streak, unlocks, coins and settings
  • the age band you chose, used only to compare your scores with others your age using built-in reference tables, and to pick a starting text size; it is never sent anywhere
  • words you saved to your Word Book
  • reminders, which are scheduled on your phone and never leave it

Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) may include this data, under Apple's or Google's terms. If you save a backup file from Settings, it goes wherever you choose to put it.

You can erase all of it at any time in Settings › Delete my data, or by deleting the app.

Speaking games

Speaking games use the microphone only while that game is on screen, and only after you allow it. Your phone's speech recognition turns what you say into text so Spry can check your answer. It runs on the phone when it can; otherwise your phone sends the audio to Apple's or Google's speech service, under their privacy terms. Spry does not record, keep or send your audio or transcripts. Speaking games are optional and can be turned off in Settings.

Advertising

Spry is free because it shows ads, provided by Google AdMob. Ads never appear during a game: at most one ad after a finished workout on some days, and videos you choose to watch for a stated reward.

To show and measure ads, Google's advertising SDK collects information such as your device's advertising identifier, approximate location based on your IP address, device and app details, ad interactions, and crash and performance data. Google's use of this information is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices:

  • iPhone: Spry asks before your advertising identifier can be used for tracking. If you tap “Ask App Not to Track”, ads are still shown but are not personalised using that identifier. You can change this in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland: Spry shows Google's consent form before any ads, and you can change your choice any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices.

Buying Remove ads turns off the after-workout ad.

Purchases

“Remove ads” is bought through the App Store or Google Play. They handle the payment; Spry never sees your payment details. Spry only learns that the purchase was made, so it can turn ads off.

Children

Spry is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Selling data

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it except with the advertising provider as described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, mention it in the app's update notes.
