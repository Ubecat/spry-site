Zunoki privacy policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Zunoki is a brain, word and math training app made by ubecat studio (“we”). This policy explains what Zunoki stores, what leaves your phone, and the choices you have.

What stays on your phone

Zunoki has no accounts. Apart from playing online with a room code or Zunoki friends (described below), it has no servers either. Everything below is stored only on your device:

  • your scores, levels, streak, unlocks, coins and settings
  • the age band you chose, used only to compare your scores with others your age using built-in reference tables, and to pick a starting text size; it is never sent anywhere
  • words you saved to your Word Book
  • reminders, which are scheduled on your phone and never leave it

Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) may include this data, under Apple's or Google's terms. If you save a backup file from Settings, it goes wherever you choose to put it.

You can erase all of it at any time in Settings › Delete my data, or by deleting the app.

Speaking games

Speaking games use the microphone only while that game is on screen, and only after you allow it. Your phone's speech recognition turns what you say into text so Zunoki can check your answer. It runs on the phone when it can; otherwise your phone sends the audio to Apple's or Google's speech service, under their privacy terms. Zunoki does not record, keep or send your audio or transcripts. Speaking games are optional and can be turned off in Settings.

Advertising

Zunoki is free because it shows ads, provided by Google AdMob. Ads never appear during a game: a short ad between games every few games, and videos you choose to watch for a stated reward.

To show and measure ads, Google's advertising SDK collects information such as your device's advertising identifier, approximate location based on your IP address, device and app details, ad interactions, and crash and performance data. Google's use of this information is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices:

  • iPhone: Zunoki asks before your advertising identifier can be used for tracking. If you tap “Ask App Not to Track”, ads are still shown but are not personalised using that identifier. You can change this in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland: Zunoki shows Google's consent form before any ads, and you can change your choice any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices.

Buying Remove ads turns off the ads between games.

Battles and Co-op with a friend

Battle a Friend and Co-op Expedition are played online with up to two other people, in one of two ways. There is never any chat.

Through Apple’s Game Center (iPhone and iPad). You invite friends, and only the friends you invite can join; they have to be your friends in Game Center first. If you choose “Add a friend”, Zunoki opens Apple’s own friend request sheet in Messages; the request goes to the person you pick through Apple, and Zunoki never sees who that is or reads your friends list. While you play, your Game Center name and your scores in that game go to the others, through Apple’s servers, so you can all see how you are doing. Zunoki stores none of it, and nothing is shared once the game is over. Apple runs Game Center under its own privacy policy.

With a room code (iPhone and Android). One player makes a room and gets a six-letter code; the others type it in. While you play, a small relay server passes your scores and game messages between the phones in the room. The relay is run for Zunoki on Cloudflare. It sees your phone’s internet address, the room code, and a play name that Zunoki gives you (two words picked from a list, like “Brave Otter”; you never type it, and you can shuffle it). It does not write the match to disk, and the room is deleted when everyone has left, or after two hours. Anyone who has the code can join while the room is open and not full, and the host sees who has joined, so share a code only with people you want to play with. Cloudflare handles connections under its own privacy policy.

Zunoki friends (optional, iPhone and Android). If you open Zunoki friends, your phone gets a random friend code and keeps a secret key; your friends list is stored only on your phone. When you add a friend by their code, a request goes into their mailbox on the same relay, and an invitation to play goes there too. A mailbox holds the sender’s friend code and play name: friend requests for up to three days, invitations for ten minutes, until the other phone collects them when Zunoki is open (there are no notifications while the app is closed). The relay stores only a fingerprint of your key, never the key itself. You can ignore or block a request, and an invitation from someone you have not accepted is thrown away. Settings › Delete my data deletes your mailbox and friend code.

Duels by turns (optional). A duel between two friends is kept on the relay for the two of you: your friend codes and play names, how each Sprig is dressed (colour, outfit, level), the five games, and each round’s score, until a week after it ends (a month if nobody plays, three days if it is never accepted). So that duels only happen between friends, the relay also keeps the friend codes on your friends list (codes only, no names). A player can only see the other’s score for a round once both have played it. If you turn notifications on, the relay also keeps a push token for your device, which Google’s Firebase Cloud Messaging uses to deliver a short message such as “your turn”. Delete my data ends your duels and deletes all of this.

Quick Match (optional). When you tap Quick Match, your phone tells the relay your age band (a number for the band you chose, not your age) and the friend codes you have blocked, and waits up to 30 seconds. The relay pairs you with someone who is looking at the same time, in your band or the one next to it, and neither of you has blocked the other. Nothing is kept after that. There is no chat: you see a play name like “Brave Otter”, how their Sprig is dressed, scores, and quick reactions and pranks from fixed lists. Settings › Play with new people turns Quick Match off, and a Block button after a match stops you being paired with that player again.

Friend streaks, nudges and the weekly board (optional). If you have Zunoki friends, your phone also tells the relay which of the last 30 days you played anything (one yes or no per day, no games or scores) how far your brain score has gone up since Monday, and how many games you played this week (for a shared weekly goal). Only people on your friends list can read this, and your phone works out streaks itself from the two lists. A friend can send you a nudge, one a day from each friend and at most five a day in all, as a short message that carries their play name. Settings › Friends see my progress turns the sharing off, and Delete my data deletes it. A prank left for a duel round (just which prank it is) is kept with the duel, like its scores. Pranks and quick reactions in a live round are passed along and not kept.

Daily leaderboards (optional)

Daily Sprint (fastest time) and Daily Rush (highest score) are ranked runs that every Zunoki player, on iPhone and Android, shares. They are off until you turn on Settings › Show me on leaderboards. If you do, your phone asks the relay for today’s problems, plays them, and sends back your answers and timing. The relay checks the answers and works out your time or score itself. It keeps your friend code, your play name (two generated words, like “Brave Otter”), how Sprig is dressed, and your result for each day, week and all time, and other players can see your play name, Sprig’s look and your result on the board. Nothing else is shown: no real name, no email, no location. You get one ranked try a day. Daily rows are deleted after about five weeks and weekly rows after about three months; your all-time best stays until you delete your data. If you press and hold a name to report it, the relay keeps that report with your friend code. Settings › Show me on leaderboards turned off, or Delete my data, removes your rows, play name and look from every board. So that a deleted phone cannot play the same day’s ranked run twice, a one-way hash of your anonymous code (it cannot be turned back into the code) is kept for about 35 days. Rankings are for fun and are checked automatically; a result that looks impossible may be removed. Expedition boards (Solo and Co-op) work differently: the app tells the relay how far an Expedition went (floors, boss stars, danger level, total score, and for Co-op how many played). The relay only checks that it looks possible, so these results are self-reported and for fun; other players can see your play name, Sprig’s look and that result. For a Co-op run your phone also sends your teammates’ friend codes and the run’s seed, and the relay keeps them for 8 days to see whether your team reported the same result. Expedition rows are kept for about three months (weekly) or until you delete your data (all time), and month boards for Daily Sprint and Daily Rush are deleted 92 days after the month ends. If you switch on Friends see my progress, friends you have added also see your brain score, your streak and your Sprig leaves (a single small note that is replaced each time and deleted when you switch it off or delete your data).

Game Center

Game Center is optional and off until you choose “Connect Game Center” in Zunoki. If you do, Zunoki sends Apple your leaderboard scores (your leaves, best streak and medal points) and the achievements you have earned, so Apple can show rankings. Apple runs Game Center under its own privacy policy. For leaderboards and achievements, Zunoki has no accounts and never sees your name or Apple ID. You can stop at any time with “Stop sharing”. Zunoki also draws a picture of your Sprig when you tap “Show off Sprig”; it is made on your phone and goes only where you choose to send it.

Feedback

“Send feedback” opens your own email app with a message to ubecatstudios@gmail.com that you can read and change before you send it. It holds the stars you chose, anything you write, and the Zunoki version. Nothing is sent unless you press Send in your email app. It is separate from any rating in the App Store or Google Play.

Purchases

“Remove ads” is bought through the App Store or Google Play. They handle the payment; Zunoki never sees your payment details. Zunoki only learns that the purchase was made, so it can turn ads off.

Children

Zunoki is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Selling data

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it except with the advertising provider as described above.
