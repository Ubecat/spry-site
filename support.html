Spry support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email ubecatstudios@gmail.com. Please say which phone and iOS version you have.

I bought Remove ads, but I still see ads

Open Settings › Restore purchase in Spry. You need to be signed in to the same Apple ID you bought it with.

How do I move Spry to a new phone?

On the old phone, open Settings › Save a backup file and keep the file somewhere you can reach it (iCloud Drive, AirDrop). On the new phone, open Settings › Restore from a file and choose it.

How do I delete my data?

Use Settings › Delete my data, or delete the app. Spry has no accounts or servers, so nothing is kept anywhere else.

The speaking games can't hear me

Check that Spry is allowed to use the microphone and speech recognition in iPhone Settings › Spry. Speaking games are optional and can be turned off in Spry's Settings.
